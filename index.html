🐭 Squeak & Seek 🐶

The world's most useless AR game: chase a rat, catch it, then YEET it.

  1. 🐭 A rat spawns on your floor and scurries around your real room.
  2. 🐕 Your pet companion floats by the camera and looks & barks toward the rat — even when it hides behind your table or pillar.
  3. 🏃 Physically move closer — the rat panics, runs, and hides under/behind detected surfaces.
  4. 👆 Corner it and TAP the rat on screen to catch it.
  5. 🚀 SWIPE UP to yeet it with glorious ragdoll physics. Repeat forever.

AR requires a WebXR device (Android Chrome + ARCore, or WebXR emulator) over HTTPS. The preview mode simulates a room with a pillar + table so you can play the whole loop on desktop.
